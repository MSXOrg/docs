# Follow MSX ways of working

Read and follow the [MSX Ways of Working](https://msxorg.github.io/docs/Ways-of-Working/).
Use that index to select every applicable standard. For work moving from an idea to delivery,
start with the [MSX Workflow](https://msxorg.github.io/docs/Ways-of-Working/Workflow/),
determine the current stage, and follow its stage procedure. The linked documentation is the
source of truth; do not restate or infer its rules here.
